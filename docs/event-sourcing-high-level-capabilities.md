## Business capabilities

- **Complete history.** Nothing is lost; the system remembers everything.
- **Time travel.** Reconstruct any past state on demand.
- **Trust and accountability.** Every change is attributable and verifiable.
- **Honest correction.** Mistakes are fixed in the open, not hidden.
- **Independent scaling of reads and writes.** Heavy reporting doesn't slow the business.
- **Purpose-built views.** Different audiences see data shaped for their needs.
- **Behavioral insight.** Understand what people do, not just where they ended up.
- **Resilience.** The system can always rebuild itself from the truth.
- **Future readiness.** New questions get answered from existing history.
- **Regulatory fit.** Audit, retention, and disclosure requirements are met by design.

## Technology directions

- **Microservices.** Clear ownership boundaries between teams and systems.
- **Event-driven architecture.** Systems integrate through events, not through tight coupling.
- **Real-time and streaming.** Live dashboards, continuous insight, instant reaction.
- **Cloud-native scale.** Each part scales to its own demand.
- **Polyglot data.** The right database for each job.
- **AI and machine learning.** Rich historical data fuels predictive capability.
- **Cross-region and edge.** Distributed operations from a single source of truth.
- **Observability.** End-to-end visibility into what the system is doing and why.
- **Workflow automation.** Long-running business processes managed explicitly.
- **Data products.** Each domain publishes its own data for others to consume.
